# Designs

Mediathek has one design, `src/concepts/mediathek.cpp`: the Home and Movies pages, the details sheet and the player stage. The kit it is built on (components, themes, the shell and the tour) is documented in the other guides here; the gallery designs of the original kit are not part of this app.

To see it: `tools/host-snapshots.sh build/snapshots mediathek`. The tour in `kTour` names the pictures: home, shelves, details, movies, playing, seek, paused.
